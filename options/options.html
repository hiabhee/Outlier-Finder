How scoring works

Outlier score = video views ÷ channel subscribers. A 10x score means the video has ten times as many views as the channel has subscribers. YouTube rounds subscriber counts, so scores are estimates. These thresholds are heuristics, not statistically validated benchmarks.

Outlier Finder reads displayed post metrics on YouTube, X and Instagram. Public profile requests omit cookies. Displayed follower counts can also be learned when you view a profile or hovercard. Everything is stored locally. Login walls, hidden counts and private profiles may prevent scoring.
