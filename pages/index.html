Outlier Finder

Compare public post metrics with creator audience size on YouTube, X and Instagram.

YouTube and public view counts use views ÷ subscribers or followers. When enabled, posts without public views use likes ÷ followers, shown as an engagement percentage. These are heuristic ratios, not predictions.

If X blocks public follower lookups, open the creator’s profile or hover their name. You can also click the unavailable score and enter the follower count you verified on their profile. Entered counts are marked as estimates.
