OUTLIER FINDER · HIABHEE

Privacy policy

Publisher: hiabhee. Updated November 10, 2026.

Outlier Finder reads public post and profile information on supported pages to calculate scores: creator names, identifiers and URLs, follower or subscriber counts, post titles, thumbnails, view and like counts, and publication information. It also processes follower counts you choose to enter.

Settings, cached public metadata, scores, recent detections, saved posts and diagnostic counts are stored in your browser using Chrome local storage. They are not sent to a developer server. The extension has no developer analytics or advertising service and does not sell this information.

On X, a bundled reader observes selected public timeline responses already loaded by the page and extracts each public post author’s username and follower count. This helps score Explore and timeline posts without visiting each author. The reader does not issue extra timeline requests, inspect request headers or authentication tokens, or process messaging endpoints. These counts stay local and detection can be disabled in Settings.

To retrieve public metadata, the extension sends HTTPS requests directly to YouTube, X or Instagram with credentials omitted. Displaying thumbnails can make requests to platform image servers. These services receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and user agent and apply their own privacy policies. The extension does not read passwords, authentication tokens, cookies or private messages.

For X growth measurements, the extension stores bounded local view-count observations and timestamps for up to 500 posts. Calculations use readings at least one minute apart within a one-hour window. Stale observations are discarded when this data is accessed; Clear cache or uninstalling removes them. No continuous background polling is performed.

Cached entries have expiry times and are refreshed or removed when accessed. Saved posts remain until removed or the extension is uninstalled. Clear cache in the popup removes transient metadata; it does not remove saved posts or settings. Remove saved items individually, or uninstall the extension to remove its local storage. No cloud account or cloud backup is provided by this extension.
