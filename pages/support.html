Support

Contact jamdadeabhishek039@gmail.com. Include the extension version, platform, public post URL, and the popup’s Data lookup message.

After loading an updated unpacked extension, click Reload in chrome://extensions, then refresh your social platform tabs.

For missing scores, check the global switch, the platform switch and badge visibility in Settings. An unavailable score means a required public metric or creator count could not be read. On X, open the creator profile or hover their name to learn the displayed follower count. Alternatively click the score and enter a verified follower count. Hidden or private metrics cannot be scored reliably.

To remove local data, delete saved items or uninstall the extension. Clear cache removes transient metadata while retaining preferences and saved items.
